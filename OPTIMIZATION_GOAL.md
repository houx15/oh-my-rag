# Continuing optimization goal

Improve source-grounded Chinese QA until further practical improvement appears
unlikely under the available data, compute, provider and privacy constraints.
C1 is the reproducible reference; promotion requires evidence, not judge agreement.
This is an ongoing research objective, not permission to deploy or publish.

Quality comes first: preserve material conditions, caps, beneficiary/time scope,
negation, supported numerical derivations and justified abstention. Unsupported
claims, fabricated facts after a refusal, and misleading scope expansion are
regressions even when quotations are valid. Report quality axes separately.
Compare p50/p95/TTFT, generation and all-in evaluation cost, invalid outputs and
operational recovery. The working +5 percentage-point adequacy / +10% p95 gate
is a practical threshold, not a power guarantee; small uncertain gains require
confirmation, and latency exceptions require an explicit justified tradeoff.

Every experiment records hypothesis, frozen code/config/source hashes, split
custody, intervention, matched baseline, results, uncertainty, failures and next
decision in `docs/optimization-log.md`. Private inputs/references/results/ledgers
stay external; reusable code/tests/docs stay in this repository. Source-review
annotations by assistants or models are never called independent human gold.
Independent model reviews use fresh contexts and blinded arm identity, but can
share provider biases. Preserve old scores and adjudication history.

Use exposed cases only for diagnostics/development. Freeze new source-family
holdouts, natural question wording, explicit source obligations and evaluation
criteria before answers. Never select or repair a candidate after inspecting its
holdout and then report that same holdout as fresh. Distinguish candidate-pool
recall from selection and generation failures. Vary one mechanism at a time.

Start with measurement repair: omission, paraphrase, negation, swapped subjects,
wrong caps, unsupported assertions and correct refusal controls. Then prioritize
condition-preserving generation, evidence selection/pool recall, and scoped
numerical reasoning as supported by observed errors. Avoid oracle reference
leakage or optimizing solely for a model judge's preference.

A practical plateau requires evidence that reasonable promising mechanisms across
observed bottlenecks fail to produce meaningful *held-out* gains, or that observed
gains are unstable under stronger evaluation and repeated baselines. Document
uncertainty, sample coverage, remaining hypotheses, and expensive/data-dependent
options. No arbitrary number of experiments establishes completion. A ceiling
score, weak judge, limited sample, publication denial, elapsed time, authorization
boundary or exhausted budget is a limitation/blocker, not a scientific plateau.

Keep the shared cumulative $100 cap, canonical ledgers, reserve-before-dispatch,
provider/privacy scope and current authorization deadlines. Record all costs,
including evaluation and failed calls. Do not reset ledgers or bypass deadlines.
No publication retry after the rejected push without accepted direct approval.
Continue safe local work despite publication being blocked. Stop only for a
user instruction, defensible scoped plateau, or a real resource/authorization
blocker; leave a reproducible next step when blocked.
